# Registration and membership

## How to join
1. Try a class: a S$29 trial at HQ. No uniform needed.
2. Register online in about 6 minutes. You pay the S$60 registration fee and your first package in the same checkout.
3. Complete the membership application form and bring 2 current photos.
4. Pick a regular dojo, called the principal dojo.

## What S$60 covers
Registration, the student handbook, your membership book, and year-one annual dues. After that, annual membership is S$48 a year.

## Experience
- No martial arts experience needed. New members begin at white belt.
- If you trained in Aikido before, bring your record book and certificates and the HQ office will verify your grade.

## Uniform
Jacket, pants and belt are required. Contact HQ a week before the first class.

## Principal dojo and training
Members train at their principal dojo. Packages bought online are valid at HQ only (see [[Policies and Terms]]).

## Concessions
Seniors and students get concession rates. Bring proof to the HQ office. See [[Fees and Packages]].
