# Policies and terms

These rules apply to every membership and package.

- **Principal dojo.** Members choose a regular dojo.
- **Membership application.** Complete the form and bring 2 current photos.
- **Compassion Guaranteed.** Missed sessions qualify for up to **2 weeks' extension** on the 1-month children's pass, no questions asked.
- **Validity extensions.** Extensions beyond the stated validity need a valid medical certificate (MC). Members can send the MC from the student portal.
- **Make-up sessions.** Students who miss their principal class may attend other time slots, subject to availability.
- **Refunds and transfers.** Training fees are non-refundable and non-transferable.
- **Package limit.** Each member may buy up to 2 training packages, in any combination.
- **Location.** Training packages are only valid at the HQ Dojo.
- **Booking.** Classes can be booked up to 1 month ahead, at least 1 day before the class.
- **Cancellation.** Cancel at least 4 hours before class to get the lesson back.

Ben must not promise a refund or an exception. Escalate any refund or exception request to the admin team.
