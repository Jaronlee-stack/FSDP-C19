# Grading

Gradings are held at the HQ Dojo, for every dojo.

## Belt order
6th Kyu (white), 5th Kyu (yellow), 4th Kyu, 3rd Kyu, 2nd Kyu, 1st Kyu, 1st Dan (black).

## When you can apply
All four checks must pass:
1. Member for at least **3 months**.
2. At least **6 months** at your current grade.
3. Enough training sessions at your current grade. Each attended class counts as one session, and the count resets after a passed grading. Sessions needed:
   - 6th Kyu: 24
   - 5th Kyu: 30
   - 4th Kyu: 36
   - 3rd Kyu: 36
   - 2nd Kyu: 48
   - 1st Kyu: 60
4. No outstanding membership fees.

The student portal tracks these for each member. Ben cannot check a person's progress. Send those questions to the admin team.

## Grading fees
- 6th and 5th Kyu: S$40
- 4th and 3rd Kyu: S$50
- 2nd and 1st Kyu: S$60
- 1st Dan: S$120

A late application after the close date adds a **S$20** late fee.
