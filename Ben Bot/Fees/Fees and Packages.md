# Fees and packages

All fees include GST. Packages are valid at the HQ Dojo only. Online payment is by card through Stripe.

## Registration and membership
- One-time registration fee: **S$60**. Includes the current year's membership, the membership book and the student handbook.
- Annual membership fee: **S$48** (current year).
- Reactivation fee: **S$60**, plus the current year's membership fee.

## Adult lesson packages (13 years and above, see [[Open Questions for Admin]])
- **10-lesson pack: S$270.** S$27 per lesson. Valid 11 weeks. Good for 1 class a week.
- **25-lesson pack: S$600.** S$24 per lesson. Valid 4 months. Most members start here.
- **50-lesson pack: S$980.** S$19.60 per lesson. Valid 6 months. For 2 or more classes a week.
- **Unlimited: S$96 every 2 weeks** (S$48 a week). Book as many classes as you like. 3-month minimum commitment.

## Children passes (4 to 12 years old, see [[Open Questions for Admin]])
- **1-month pass: S$150.** 4 lessons. Valid 5 weeks from the first session.
- **6-month pass: S$139 per month**, or **S$834 per semester**. 24 lessons. Semester-based billing.
- **50-lesson pack: S$1,200 per semester**, or S$24 per lesson. Valid 6 months from the first session.

## Concessions
- **Seniors aged 65 and above: 40% off.**
- **Full-time students and NSFs aged 17 to 25: 50% off.**
- Bring proof of age, student status or NS to the HQ office. The concession is applied to the account.

## Limits
- Each member may hold up to **2 training packages** at a time, in any combination.

## Trial and guest fees
See [[Trials and Guest Passes]].

## Uniform
Uniform (jacket, pants and belt) is required for members. Contact HQ a week before the first class to arrange delivery to your dojo. The uniform price is not in this vault yet (see [[Open Questions for Admin]]).
