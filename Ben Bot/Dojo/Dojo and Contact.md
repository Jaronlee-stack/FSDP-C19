# Dojo and contact

## Headquarters (HQ) Dojo
- Name: ASK Headquarters Dojo (Aikido Shinju-Kai)
- Address: Thomson V One, 9 Sin Ming Road, #B1-02, Singapore 575630
- Region: Central
- Dojo code: ASK HQ
- The HQ Dojo is the only dojo where online booking is available. Gradings are held here.
- Opened as a permanent headquarters and Shinju Hub in May 2024, with two training halls, a member lounge and a cafe.

## Office hours (HQ office)
- Monday to Friday: 10 AM to 9 PM
- Saturday and Sunday: 8 AM to 6 PM
