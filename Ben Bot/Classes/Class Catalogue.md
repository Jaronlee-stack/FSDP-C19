## What Aikido is (for new visitors)
A Japanese martial art of throws, joint locks and pins, trained alongside the sword, staff and knife. There are no tournaments. Members are promoted by showing understanding of techniques, which grow more demanding with each grade. Benefits: fitness, focus, self-defence, confidence and discipline.
