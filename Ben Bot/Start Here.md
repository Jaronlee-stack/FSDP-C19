# Ben Bot knowledge vault

Ben is the Aikido Shinju-Kai dojo assistant. This vault is what Ben knows. Anything not written here, Ben does not know yet.

## Ben answers from these notes
- [[Dojo and Contact]]: HQ address, hours, phone, email, which dojos are open
- [[Class Catalogue]]: every class and programme, who it is for, how long it runs
- [[HQ Weekly Timetable]]: HQ's weekly classes (online booking covers HQ only)
- [[Fees and Packages]]: registration, dues, lesson packages, concessions
- [[Trials and Guest Passes]]: S$29 trial, guest passes, private classes
- [[Registration and Membership]]: how to join, what the one-off fee covers, uniform
- [[Policies and Terms]]: package limits, validity, extensions, refunds
- [[Grading]]: eligibility, grading fees, belt order
- [[Venues Overview]]: the community dojos (term courses, registered through the club or instructor)

## How Ben learns
- [[Ben Operating Rules]]: when Ben answers, when Ben escalates, what Ben never does
- [[FAQ Index]]: questions answered often, written up as notes (Ben proposes, admin approves)
- [[Unsure Questions Index]]: questions Ben was not confident about, with status
- [[Admin Inbox]]: escalations waiting for an admin reply in the admin portal
- [[Knowledge Changelog]]: every change to a knowledge note, with date and source
- [[Open Questions for Admin]]: conflicts between sources and gaps to fill first

## Notes on status
Each knowledge note has a `status` in its properties:
- `confirmed`: taken from the public website or the fee terms; Ben may quote it
- `needs-confirmation`: Ben may say it is "to be confirmed" and must offer to check with HQ
- `sample`: placeholder data; Ben must never quote it
