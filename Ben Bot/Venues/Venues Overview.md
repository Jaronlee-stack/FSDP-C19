# Venues overview

Shinju-Kai runs its regular classes at the HQ Dojo, and at community dojos across Singapore.

## How community dojos work
- They run **term courses** through the club. Schedules change each term.
- Register through the club or your instructor.
- **Class times are not in this vault.** Ben must not quote times for any community dojo. Ben should say the HQ office will give the current class times and the next intake: +65 6513 1588 or hq@aikidoshinjukai.com.sg.
- Training packages bought online are valid at HQ only.
- Gradings are held at HQ.
