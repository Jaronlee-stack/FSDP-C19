Yudansha Additional Scoring Requirements (Dan Points)
To maintain structural growth, all black belt ranks must obtain a minimum 3-point average per year by attending HQ-endorsed workshops, seminars, and events.
1.0 Point: Attending any seminar or event organized directly by the Aikido Shinju-Kai Headquarters.
2.5 Points: Participating in official international seminars, external camps, or headquarters-sanctioned trips.
0.5 Point: Attending internal self-improvement programs, enrichment classes, or specific weapon training workshops.
Important: Accumulated points will reset back to zero immediately upon upgrading to the next Dan rank.
