By signing this form, I confirm my membership application and certify that I understand and agree to all the stated Terms and Conditions. I also declare that the personal data I have provided is true and correct.

1. Indemnity

I accept personal responsibility and agree that Aikido Shinju-Kai (ASK), Shinju-Kai International Pte Ltd (SIPL), its affiliated organisations, partners and governing bodies, their officers, instructors and personnel, other members, participants, supervisors, coaches and sponsoring organisations or their agents will not be held liable for any loss or damage, including injury, death or damage to property, caused or alleged to be caused in whole or in part by the negligence of myself or my child or otherwise.

2. Training of my own free will

I recognise and accept that I enter martial arts training entirely of my own free will, and I understand the importance of following the rules of training and dojo etiquette.

3. Health declaration

I certify that I am, or my child is, in good physical condition with no disease, injury or other condition that would impair performance or physical and mental well-being during training. If I have any injury or illness that could affect my ability to take part, I will tell the organisation and instructor, and discuss with my doctor whether martial arts training is appropriate.

4. Emergency medical treatment

In an emergency injury that needs medical attention, I permit a doctor, nurse, athletic trainer or other emergency medical personnel to give me or my child medical assistance or treatment. The cost of any treatment or hospitalisation arising from injury during training will not be paid by ASK, its affiliated organisations and governing bodies, their officers, instructors or personnel.

5. Fees

Membership and registration fees are non-refundable. All Principal Dojo training fees must be paid before the start of a new term. Annual dues are payable each year; a membership unpaid for more than 365 days lapses, and class booking is locked until dues are paid.

6. Private policy

In accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2012, I consent to the collection, use and disclosure of my personal data by ASK and/or any ASK-Authorised Party for membership-related matters.

7. Terminology

  1. "ASK" means Aikido Shinju-Kai.
  2. "Data" means any information relating to me in the possession of, or provided from time to time to, any ASK-Authorised Party.
  3. "ASK-Authorised Party" means Aikido Shinju-Kai in China or its related companies, such as Shinju-Kai International Pte Ltd in Singapore; the Company's existing or potential third-party service providers, affiliated organisations, contracting partners and agents; any governmental or regulatory authority in any jurisdiction; and any other party listed in the prevailing ASK Privacy Policy.
  4. The current ASK Privacy Policy is published at aikidoshinjukai.com.sg.

8. Signature

Applicants below 21 years old must have the form signed by a parent or guardian, who accepts these terms on the applicant's behalf.

9. Right to reject

Aikido Shinju-Kai reserves the right to reject any application without giving a reason.
