Terms & conditions

Good to know

The rules that apply to every membership and package.

  • Principal dojo. Members choose a regular dojo, called the principal dojo.
  • Membership application. Complete the membership application form and bring 2 current photos.
  • Compassion Guaranteed. Missed sessions qualify for up to 2 weeks' extension on the 1-month training pass, no questions asked.
  • Validity extensions. Extensions beyond the stated validity need a valid medical certificate. Members can send their MC from the student portal.
  • Make-up sessions. Students who miss their principal class may attend other time slots, subject to availability.
  • Refunds and transfers. Training fees are non-refundable and non-transferable.
  • Package limit. Each member may buy up to 2 training packages, in any combination.
  • Location. Training packages are only valid at the Shinju-Kai Headquarters.

Uniform (jacket, pants and belt) is required for members; contact HQ a week before your first class to arrange delivery to your dojo. Grading fees are listed in the student portal.
