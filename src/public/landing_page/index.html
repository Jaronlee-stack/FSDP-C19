Benefits of Aikido

A martial way, not a contest

Aikido is a Japanese martial art of throws, joint locks and pins, trained alongside the sword, staff and knife. Founder Morihei Ueshiba, known as O Sensei, built it from years of studying older martial systems.

There are no tournaments. You are promoted by showing understanding of the techniques, which grow more demanding with each grade. Partners blend with an attack and redirect its energy, aiming to resolve conflict without harm.

What sets Shinju-Kai apart: strong technique, certified instructors and Philip Lee Shihan's clear teaching method, with standard grading requirements, seminars and instructor certification across every dojo.

体

Physical

All-round fitness, flexibility and relaxation. Whole-body movement instead of isolated muscles.

心

Psychological

Body and mind are trained together. Physical calm becomes mental calm; confidence shows in how you move.

護

Self-defence

Techniques scale from gentle control to firm counters, so they fit a wide range of real situations.

道

Daily life

Facing conflict on the mat teaches you to meet everyday challenges calmly and constructively.

Our history

From one dojo at SAFRA Tampines

Shinju-Kai began in 1988 with a single dojo and four pioneer instructors. It now runs a permanent headquarters, dozens of local dojos and affiliated dojos in China, Malaysia and Indonesia.

Vision & mission

What we work towards

Everything from instructor certification to children's programmes follows from these.

Mission

Careers

The Deshi programme

A Deshi (disciple) trains at the HQ daily, usually two classes a day, and helps run the dojo. Deshis train about three times as much as regular students and learn teaching and administration along the way.

Who can apply

  • Men and women aged 18 to 40
  • Healthy and able to train daily
  • Own health insurance in good standing
  • Able to support yourself for the term
  • At least 1st Kyu (black-gold belt)
  • Recommendation from your instructor

What you'll do

  • Train at least twice a day at HQ
  • Assist instructors in class
  • Look after the training halls
  • Help with admin and projects
  • Represent Shinju-Kai at events

How to apply

Email the International Affairs Department with a cover letter, your grade and training history, your instructor's recommendation and how long you'd like to train.
